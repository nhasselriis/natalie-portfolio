Audio Project

Shut Up and Take My Podcast

Shut Up and Take My Podcast is a Futurama-obsessed deep dive into the sci-fi satire that’s 40% comedy, 60% existential dread. We break down episodes, characters, lore, and dumb jokes that somehow make us feel things. Grab a Slurm, hop in the tube, and let’s scream about the future—together. 🚀🧠

Illustration & Writing

Fountain Pen Comic Strip

Some people collect superheroes—I collect fountain pens. Every pen has its own personality, from the elegant classics to the budget workhorses that try just as hard. One day I started imagining what those personalities would look like if they were actual characters, and before long the idea grew into a comic strip. It's a fun way to blend my love of writing, drawing, and fountain pens into stories that fellow pen enthusiasts can hopefully laugh along with.

Visual Art

Anastasia Art Piece

The 1997 animated film Anastasia has always held a special place in my heart. Inspired by one of the movie's most iconic scenes, the upcoming coloured-pencil drawing focuses on Anastasia's beautiful blue dress and the elegance that made the character so memorable. It's my way of paying tribute to a film that continues to inspire my love of traditional art and animation.
