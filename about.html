About the Human

Meet Natalie

Natalie is a novelist, visual artist, and storyteller driven by a lifelong passion for creating meaningful, immersive worlds. Blending elements of fantasy and introspective fiction, her work explores resilience, identity, and the quiet strength found in overcoming adversity.

Having created art since early childhood, Natalie works across traditional and digital mediums, with a particular love for detailed illustration, photography, and handcrafted pieces like origami.

She is currently revising her debut novel, Archway, a project over a decade in the making, while also building a creative platform to share her work and connect with others through storytelling.

When she is not writing, Natalie enjoys gaming, streaming, caring for animals, and finding inspiration in everyday moments.

Beyond the Keyboard

Creative Interests & Hobbies

The hobbies and creative practices that inspire my writing, art, streaming, and everyday life.

Video Games

Video games have been a part of Natalie's life since she was three years old. Her disability never stopped her from playing video games, even if she has to do it one-handed.

Reading

Reading has always been a passion of Natalie's, providing her with escapism and new perspectives. She enjoys a variety of genres, from science fiction to historical fiction.

Cooking & Baking

When living with some friends back in 2020, Natalie ventured more into the foray of cooking and baking and had a sweet spot for cakes and making unique artistic ones. She has a tradition of baking a custom artistic cake yearly for one of her friends, with the newest one being made soon.

Art

Art has been a huge part of Natalie's life ever since she was young. Her father is an artist and musician, and she definitely took after the artistry, but isn't as musically inclined. She prefers colored pencil above everything else and taught herself origami back in 2015.

LEGO

Never having built LEGO before, Natalie built her first Lego set at 31, at the start of 2025. Now, in mid-2026, she has built several more sets and continues to collect them.

Streaming

Natalie began streaming prominently in mid-2025, during a rough patch in her life. She streamed every single day for eight months before taking a hiatus for her mental and physical health. Nowadays, in mid-2026, she streams most days for one to two hours and now goes by the handle of LadyKida. She's currently doing a new playthrough of The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom.
